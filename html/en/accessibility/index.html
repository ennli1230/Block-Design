Accessibility

Information about website accessibility

This is a local frontend preview. Company details and legal text must be verified and approved by the actual operator before launch.

Our approach

The pages aim to support keyboard use, readable text and different screen sizes. The scope of Türkiye’s 2025/10 accessibility circular for Group A agencies will be assessed once the real agency group is verified. Full compliance will only be claimed after testing with assistive technologies.

Feedback

If you cannot use any content or function, please contact us through the contact page. Actual company contact details will be verified before launch.

Ongoing work

New content and documents added through the future admin panel will be checked for headings, alternative text and readability before publication.

Report an access issue

If you encounter a keyboard, screen reader, zoom or contrast issue, tell us the page and step through the contact page. Forms are previews until the backend is ready.
