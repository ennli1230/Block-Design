About Scala Nova

A private travel brand rooted in Kuşadası, connecting local knowledge with thoughtful journeys across the Aegean.

Rooted in Kuşadası. Built for personal travel.

Scala Nova is being developed as a premium private travel brand for Kuşadası and the Aegean region, connected to the operating foundation of Castle Turizm.

The service approach is simple: clear planning, private pacing, professional local coordination and one continuous conversation from first enquiry to final farewell.

Local knowledge

Routes shaped from practical knowledge of Kuşadası and the surrounding ancient region.

Private by design

Experiences planned for individuals, families and small private groups.

Professional foundation

The brand is intended to operate with Castle Turizm travel-agency infrastructure.

Who is About Scala Nova for?

A private travel brand rooted in Kuşadası, connecting local knowledge with thoughtful journeys across the Aegean. Suitability, time and priorities are reviewed with the request.

How is About Scala Nova planned?

Date, starting point, party size and the main interest provide the first outline.

Can the programme be adapted?

Transport, walking and the order of stops can be considered around the party within practical limits.

Where are price and availability confirmed?

Price, availability and final programme details are confirmed after the date, starting point and party information are shared.

Which details are confirmed after a request?

The starting point, date, party size, route priorities and any specific needs are confirmed while the programme is prepared.
